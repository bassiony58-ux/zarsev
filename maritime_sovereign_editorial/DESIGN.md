## Brand & Style

This design system expresses a **Clean Luxury International Corporate** identity tailored for global maritime commerce, terminal management, and cross-border trade logistics. It balances high-stakes institutional trustworthiness with an understated, editorial luxury inspired by modern architectural port pavilions and high-end nautical engineering.

### Personality & Values
- **Sovereign Reliability:** Solid, immovable authority rooted in oceanic logistics and customs clearance precision.
- **Airy Precision:** Uncluttered, expansive layouts that treat complex operational data as museum-grade typography.
- **Restrained Prestige:** Luxury conveyed through generous negative space, crisp hairline borders, and selective muted gold accents rather than decorative flourishes.

### Aesthetic Paradigm
The visual direction fuses **Corporate Modernism** with an **Editorial Minimalist** sensibility. Interfaces rely on expansive canvas planes, razor-thin structured dividers, rich deep navy typography, and calm oceanic teal accents. The feeling is calm, institutional, and globally elite.

## Colors

The palette mirrors deep-sea nautical authority paired with bright offshore sunlight and refined architectural masonry.

### Color Roles & Application
- **Deep Navy (`#172D46`):** Primary brand anchor. Applied to top-tier headings, primary CTAs, main navigation shells, and high-impact structural panels. Conveys institutional stability.
- **Ocean Teal (`#397D83`):** Secondary action driver. Governs interactive states, key task buttons, operational links, and telemetry accents.
- **Muted Gold (`#C5A46D`):** Tertiary status indicator. Used strictly in micro-doses (tier tags, certified customs seals, high-priority status indicators, subtle accent rules). Never for large surface fills.
- **Slate Body Neutral (`#344054`):** Engineered for sustained readability across dense customs declarations, freight metrics, and contracts.
- **Subtle Border (`#E4E7EC`):** The structural line work across the entire system. Replaces heavy shadows with crisp definition.
- **Surface Canvas (`#F7F8FA`) & Card Plane (`#FFFFFF`):** Two-tier background layering providing soft warmth and contrast without glare.

## Typography

Typography relies on **IBM Plex Sans** (incorporating its paired native Arabic script companion **IBM Plex Sans Arabic**). This typeface delivers a technical, architectural rigor appropriate for seaport administration while remaining warm and editorial.

### Rules of Engagement
- **Line Height:** Body copy maintains a generous 1.6x–1.7x multiplier to ensure long-form manifests, contracts, and service descriptions breathe naturally.
- **Display Presence:** Large titles feature subtle negative tracking (`-0.015em` to `-0.02em`) to deliver corporate gravitas.
- **Labels & Micro-data:** Micro-labels and metadata captions use uppercase typesetting with expanded tracking (`0.06em` to `0.08em`) to mirror luxury freight documentation stamps.
- **Bilingual Rhythm:** In mixed English/Arabic layouts, maintain line-height values at their specified heights without collapsing, preserving vertical baseline alignment across scripts.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built upon a **12-column structured fluid grid** with an intentional focus on asymmetry and generous white space. 

### Breakpoints & Composition
- **Desktop (1280px+):** 12 columns, 32px (`2rem`) gutters, 64px (`4rem`) outer margin. Maximum layout bounding box capped at 1440px to retain editorial balance.
- **Tablet (768px - 1279px):** 8 columns, 24px (`1.5rem`) gutters, 32px (`2rem`) outer margin.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4 columns, 16px (`1rem`) gutters, 20px (`1.25rem`) outer margin.

### Spatial Discipline
Sections must feature intentional vertical breathing room. Hero bands and primary structural cards should avoid dense stacking; components prioritize internal padding over compact heights to project confidence and calm authority.

## Elevation & Depth

To sustain a clean luxury aesthetic, this design system avoids heavy drop shadows and fuzzy ambient spreads. Elevation is rendered through **tonal layering and hairline boundaries**.

### Surface Hierarchy
1. **Canvas Layer (`#F7F8FA`):** The structural foundation behind dashboards, directory lists, and content frames.
2. **Elevated Card Plane (`#FFFFFF`):** Interactive cards, tables, and modal layers sit as crisp flat slabs. Bordered with a hairline rule: `1px solid #E4E7EC`.
3. **Subtle Operational Elevation:** When dragging, floating, or lifting an active element (e.g., vessel tracking card, popover), apply an ultra-diffused shadow tinted with Deep Navy:
   - `0 12px 32px -4px rgba(23, 45, 70, 0.06), 0 4px 12px -2px rgba(23, 45, 70, 0.03)`
4. **Dividers & Structural Gridlines:** Pure structural lines use `#E4E7EC` at `1px` width. Avoid double outlines or dark dividing grooves.

## Shapes

The geometric framework is **Soft (`1`)**, emphasizing precise engineering, architectural structuralism, and corporate discipline.

### Geometry Hierarchy
- **Standard UI Elements (`0.25rem` / 4px):** Inputs, standard buttons, tabular data rows, segmented controls, status badges.
- **Panels & Cards (`rounded-lg` / 0.5rem / 8px):** Primary container blocks, seaport service cards, operational summary modules.
- **Flyouts & Modals (`rounded-xl` / 0.75rem / 12px):** Dialog windows, export manifest overlays, tracking inspect drawers.
- **Circles (`full`):** Reserved exclusively for user avatars, vessel status indicators, and numbered step nodes. Never use pill-shaped buttons.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Deep Navy (`#172D46`) fill, white text, 4px border radius. Hover state transitions subtly to `#112133`. Active state scales to `0.99`.
- **Secondary / Action:** Ocean Teal (`#397D83`) fill, white text, 4px border radius. Used for actionable transactional paths (e.g., "Book Berthing Slot", "Submit Manifest"). Hover state transitions to `#2E666B`.
- **Ghost / Outlined:** Transparent background, `1px solid #E4E7EC`, `#172D46` text. Hover state introduces `#F7F8FA` fill and darkens border to `#172D46`.
- **Button Sizing:** Standard height `44px` with horizontal padding of `20px` (`space-md`), keeping touch areas reliable while maintaining sleek proportions.

### Cards & Panels
- **Structure:** Clean white (`#FFFFFF`) surface, `1px solid #E4E7EC` border, `8px` corner radius.
- **Padding:** Minimum `32px` (`2rem`) internal padding on desktop; `20px` on mobile.
- **Featured / Tiered Variant:** Subtle `2px` top accent border using Muted Gold (`#C5A46D`) or Ocean Teal (`#397D83`) to signify high-priority tier services without overwhelming the card body.

### Chips & Badges
- **Status Badges:** Text in `label-sm` uppercase. Solid or tint backgrounds with 4px radius:
  - *Cleared / Active:* Background `#EBF5F5`, text `#397D83`.
  - *Certified / VIP:* Background `#FAF6EF`, text `#8F7347` (derived from Muted Gold).
  - *Neutral / In-Transit:* Background `#F2F4F7`, text `#344054`.

### Form Fields & Inputs
- **Base Style:** Background `#FFFFFF`, height `48px`, border `1px solid #E4E7EC`, radius `4px`, font `body-md`.
- **Focus State:** Border changes to Ocean Teal (`#397D83`) with a soft glow ring: `0 0 0 3px rgba(57, 125, 131, 0.15)`. No default browser outlines.
- **Labels:** Positioned outside input, set in `label-md` with neutral slate (`#344054`) tone.

### Checkboxes & Radios
- **Style:** `18px` by `18px` square (`3px` radius for checkbox, circular for radio). Border `1.5px solid #D0D5DD`.
- **Selected State:** Filled with Deep Navy (`#172D46`) featuring a crisp white SVG check or inner pip.

### Data Tables & Manifest Lists
- **Structure:** Transparent header bar on `#F7F8FA` with uppercase `label-sm` text. Rows set on `#FFFFFF` with `1px solid #E4E7EC` bottom border only.
- **Hover:** Subtle tint background `#FAFBFC` on row hover. No alternating zebra stripes.

### Specialized Maritime Components
- **Vessel & Berth Tracking Tracker:** Horizontal route milestone indicator using `#E4E7EC` connection hairlines and `#397D83` active node states.
- **Metric Stat Block:** Massive numeric data point (`display-xl` in `#172D46`) crowned by a tracked uppercase subtitle (`label-sm` in `#397D83`), framed with generous padding.
